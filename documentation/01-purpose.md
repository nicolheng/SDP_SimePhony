# 1. Purpose

## The problem

After handover, defects in new homes (leaks, cracks, faulty sockets, hollow tiles…) are handled one ticket at a time:

- **The same defect keeps coming back.** A ceiling leak is patched and closed, then returns after the next rain,
  because the source (often in the unit above) was never found.
- **Shared causes go unnoticed.** When several units in the same stack have the same problem, nobody connects them.
- **Manual work slows everything down.** Staff read each report, work out the trade needed, find a free technician
  and chase buyers for missing details.
- **Buyers are left guessing.** Updates are irregular, and buyers sign off without knowing what to check.

## Who it is for

| User | What they need |
|---|---|
| **Buyer** (homeowner) | Report a defect quickly with a photo, see progress, book a repair time, check the repair and sign off |
| **Technician / subcontractor** | Clear jobs with the right details, accept or hand over a visit, record the result and the repair |
| **SDP staff** | See every case, spot recurring and clustered defects, approve extra inspections, sign off repairs |

## Goal

Close the **problem**, not just the ticket: fix defects faster, catch recurring and stack-wide issues early,
and keep a human in charge of the important decisions.
