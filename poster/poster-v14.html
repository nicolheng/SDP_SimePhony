Simephony
People Challenge

Don’t just close the defect.Prove the root cause is fixed.

Simephony is an agentic resolution layer that verifies every repair and spots recurring defects early, connecting buyers, Sime Darby Property and contractors through one trusted record.

4
Innovation
What tickets can’t do
01 Similar-Defect Linking
Links related defects across units, stacks and contractors to spot systemic issues.
02 Recurrence Detection
Auto-escalates when recurring defects or affected units hit set thresholds.
03 Human-in-the-Loop
AI investigates and recommends. Humans approve verdicts, sign-offs and escalations.
04 Evidence & Guardrails
Traceable records, duplicate repair-photo detection, restricted unsafe AI replies.
